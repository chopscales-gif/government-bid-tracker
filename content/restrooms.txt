title: Restroom Plumbing Fixture Replacement, Town Hall and Library
agency: Town of Quarry Bend
number: IFB 27-04
type: Invitation for Bids
issued: March 2, 2027

## 1. Work
The Town of Quarry Bend invites bids to replace the plumbing fixtures in the public restrooms at Town Hall, 10 Granary Road, and the Quarry Bend Library, 22 Granary Road, Quarry Bend, Utah: 14 toilets, 6 urinals and 12 sinks, with sensor-operated flush valves and faucets, new stop valves, and repair of wall and floor finishes disturbed by the work. All fixtures must meet accessibility requirements.

The Town's estimate is $65,000. NAICS code 238220.

## 2. Who may bid
The bidder must hold a Utah S210 General Plumbing Contractor license. Under the Town's Small Purchase Policy, purchases under $75,000 are limited to small businesses with fewer than 100 employees.

## 3. Dates
The buildings may be visited during business hours without an appointment. Questions are due by Wednesday, March 17, 2027. Bids are due by 2:00 p.m. on Wednesday, March 24, 2027 at the Town Clerk's office, 10 Granary Road. Bids may be delivered or sent by email.

The work must be done one restroom at a time so that each building always has a restroom in service, and must be finished by May 28, 2027.

## 4. Bid contents
The Bid Form with a price for each building, the fixture schedule showing the make and model of each fixture offered, a copy of the contractor license, and the small business certification on the Bid Form. No bid security is required. A performance bond is required only if the contract price is more than $50,000.
