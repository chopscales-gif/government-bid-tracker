## 1. General information
This is a combined synopsis and solicitation for construction services. The Federal Facilities Service, Mountain Region, intends to award one firm-fixed-price contract to replace the pneumatic HVAC controls at the Ridgeview Outpatient Clinic, 2400 Ridgeview Parkway, Ridgeview, Utah, with a direct digital control system, including new controllers, valves, actuators, sensors and operator workstation.

The magnitude of construction is between $250,000 and $500,000.

## 2. Set-aside
This acquisition is set aside 100 percent for service-disabled veteran-owned small businesses. The NAICS code is 238220 and the small business size standard is $19 million. Offers from firms that are not certified service-disabled veteran-owned small businesses will not be considered.

## 3. Site visit
One organized site visit will be held on Thursday, March 11, 2027 at 9:00 a.m. Offerors are urged to attend. Requests for information are due by Tuesday, March 16, 2027 at 4:00 p.m. Mountain Time.

## 4. Offers
Offers are due by 2:00 p.m. Mountain Time on Tuesday, March 30, 2027 and must be submitted by email to the Contracting Officer. An offer must include the completed price schedule, a bid guarantee of 20 percent of the offered price, three past performance references for controls projects, and the completed representations and certifications.

Performance and payment bonds are required within ten days of award. The period of performance is 150 days from the notice to proceed.
