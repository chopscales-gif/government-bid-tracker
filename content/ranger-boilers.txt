## 1. Requirement
The Federal Facilities Service, Mountain Region, requires annual inspection, cleaning, combustion tuning and minor repair of five hot water boilers at the Aspen Hollow Administrative Site, 12 Forest Road 40, Aspen Hollow, Utah. The contract will have a base year and four option years.

The estimated value, including all option years, is $160,000.

## 2. Set-aside
This requirement is set aside for women-owned small businesses. Quotations will be accepted only from firms certified as women-owned small businesses. NAICS code 238220, size standard $19 million.

## 3. Quotations
Quotations are due by 4:00 p.m. Mountain Time on Friday, March 19, 2027, by email to the Contracting Officer. A quotation must include a price for the base year and each option year, a description of the firm's boiler service experience, and the names and certifications of the technicians who will do the work. No site visit is planned.

## 4. Basis of award
Award will be made to the responsible firm whose quotation is the lowest in price and is technically acceptable.

Contracting Officer: Delphine Okafor, delphine.okafor@ffs-mountain.example.
