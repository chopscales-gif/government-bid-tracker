title: Boiler Replacement, Larkmoor Elementary School
agency: Cedar Bench School District
number: IFB 27-014
type: Invitation for Bids
issued: February 16, 2027

## 1. Project
Cedar Bench School District invites sealed bids from qualified mechanical contractors to remove two existing cast iron hot water boilers at Larkmoor Elementary School, 410 Orchard Lane, Cedar Bench, Utah, and to furnish and install two high-efficiency condensing boilers with new primary pumps, venting, gas piping and controls, as shown in the drawings and specifications.

The engineer's estimate for this work is $380,000 to $450,000. This procurement falls under NAICS code 238220, Plumbing, Heating, and Air-Conditioning Contractors. It is open to all qualified bidders. There is no set-aside.

## 2. Schedule
A pre-bid walk-through of the boiler room will be held on Thursday, March 4, 2027 at 10:00 a.m. at the school's main office. Attendance is encouraged but is not required.

Written questions must be received by the Purchasing Office by 5:00 p.m. on Wednesday, March 10, 2027. Questions received after that time will not be answered.

Sealed bids are due at the District Purchasing Office, 1200 Bench Road, Cedar Bench, no later than 2:00 p.m. on Thursday, March 18, 2027. Bids will be opened and read aloud at that time. Late bids will be returned unopened.

All work must take place during the summer recess. Work may begin on June 7, 2027 and must reach substantial completion by August 6, 2027. Liquidated damages of $500 per calendar day apply after that date.

## 3. Bidder qualifications
The bidder must hold a current Utah S350 HVAC Contractor license at the time of bid. The bidder must have completed at least three boiler replacement projects of similar size in occupied public buildings within the last five years, and must list them on the Bidder Experience Form.

## 4. What to submit
Each bid must be submitted in a sealed envelope marked with the bidder's name and "IFB 27-014 Boiler Replacement" and must contain:

(a) the Bid Form, signed by an officer of the company, with the lump sum price written in both words and figures;

(b) the Bidder Experience Form;

(c) a copy of the bidder's Utah contractor license;

(d) acknowledgment of every addendum issued, in the space provided on the Bid Form.

Bids sent by fax or email will not be accepted. The apparent low bidder must provide a list of all subcontractors within 24 hours of the bid opening.

## 5. Bonds and insurance
Bid security is not required for this project. The successful bidder must furnish performance and payment bonds, each for 100 percent of the contract price, within ten days of the notice of award, together with a certificate of insurance showing commercial general liability coverage of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence.

## 6. Other conditions
Removal of asbestos-containing insulation on the existing boilers will be completed by the District under a separate contract before June 7 and is not part of this work. All workers on school property must pass a background check under District Policy 3120 before starting work.

## 7. Contact
Direct all questions in writing to Imogen Tavares, Purchasing Agent, at purchasing@cedarbench.example. Bidders may not contact the engineer or school staff about this solicitation.
