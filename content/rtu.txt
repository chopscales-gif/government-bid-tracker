## 1. Work
Alder Fork School District will accept sealed bids to replace six packaged gas/electric rooftop units, from 7.5 to 15 tons, serving the classroom wings of Sego Ridge Middle School, 1890 Sego Ridge Drive, Alder Fork, Utah. The work includes curb adapters, gas and electrical reconnection, new thermostats tied to the District's control system, crane service, and disposal of the old units.

Estimated construction cost: $210,000 to $260,000. NAICS 238220. Open competition, no set-aside.

## 2. Key dates
Optional site visit: Wednesday, March 10, 2027, 3:30 p.m., after school dismissal. Meet at the front office.

Last day for questions: Monday, March 15, 2027.

Bids due: Tuesday, March 23, 2027, 1:00 p.m., District Office, 300 Main Street, Alder Fork.

Work period: June 1 to July 30, 2027.

## 3. Who may bid
Bidders must hold a Utah S350 HVAC Contractor license and must have carried out rooftop unit replacements at two or more schools or similar public buildings in the past four years.

## 4. Bid package
A complete bid consists of: the Bid Form; a bid bond or cashier's check for 5 percent of the bid; the Reference Sheet listing the projects that meet Section 3; a copy of the contractor license; and the signed Addenda Acknowledgment.

The equipment schedule names an approved manufacturer. Requests to substitute another manufacturer must be made in writing at least seven days before the bid date.

## 5. After award
The contractor must supply performance and payment bonds for 100 percent of the contract and a certificate of insurance before the contract is signed. All workers must complete the District's background check.
