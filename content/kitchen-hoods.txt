## 1. Project
Cedar Bench School District invites sealed bids to replace the kitchen exhaust hoods, exhaust fans and gas-fired make-up air units in the kitchens of Larkmoor Elementary, Hillcrest Elementary and Cedar Bench Junior High School. The work includes new stainless steel hoods, connection to the existing fire suppression systems by a licensed fire suppression subcontractor, ductwork, and air balancing.

The engineer's estimate is $180,000. NAICS code 238220. There is no set-aside.

## 2. Schedule
A walk-through of all three kitchens will start at Larkmoor Elementary at 3:30 p.m. on Tuesday, March 16, 2027. Attendance is not required.

Written questions are due by 5:00 p.m. on Monday, March 22, 2027.

Sealed bids are due at the District Purchasing Office, 1200 Bench Road, Cedar Bench, no later than 2:00 p.m. on Tuesday, March 30, 2027.

Work must be done between June 7 and July 23, 2027.

## 3. Bidder qualifications
The bidder must hold a current Utah S350 HVAC Contractor license. The bidder must name its fire suppression subcontractor on the Bid Form.

## 4. What to submit
The Bid Form signed by an officer of the company; bid security of 5 percent of the bid price; a copy of the bidder's Utah contractor license; and acknowledgment of every addendum.

## 5. Bonds and insurance
The successful bidder must furnish performance and payment bonds for 100 percent of the contract price and a certificate of insurance within ten days of the notice of award. All workers on school property must pass a background check under District Policy 3120.
