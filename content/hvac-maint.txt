## 1. Overview
The City of Marlow Springs is seeking proposals from mechanical service contractors to provide scheduled preventive maintenance and on-call repair of heating, ventilating and air-conditioning equipment at eleven city buildings, including City Hall, the Public Safety Building, two fire stations and the Senior Center. An equipment list is attached as Exhibit A.

The City has budgeted approximately $95,000 per year for this service. The applicable NAICS code is 238220. Any qualified firm may submit a proposal.

## 2. Term
The contract term is three years beginning July 1, 2027. The City may renew the contract for up to two additional one-year terms.

## 3. Service requirements
The contractor must perform preventive maintenance on each item of equipment four times per year, following Exhibit B. For on-call repairs the contractor must have a technician on site within four hours of a call during business hours, and within eight hours at other times, every day of the year.

All technicians assigned to the contract must hold EPA Section 608 certification. The contractor must hold a Utah S350 HVAC Contractor license.

## 4. Proposal contents
Proposals must not be longer than 20 pages, not counting resumes and required forms, and must include:

1. a cover letter signed by a person authorized to commit the firm;

2. a description of the firm and of the service team, with a resume for the proposed lead technician;

3. three references from public agencies or commercial property owners for maintenance contracts held in the last three years, with a contact name and telephone number for each;

4. the Price Form (Exhibit C), giving an annual price for preventive maintenance and hourly rates for repair work;

5. a sample preventive maintenance report from a current customer;

6. a certificate of insurance. The required coverage is set out in the sample contract, Exhibit D.

## 5. How proposals are scored
Proposals will be scored by a committee: qualifications and experience of the team, 35 points; approach to the work and response times, 25 points; price, 30 points; references, 10 points. The City may interview the highest scoring firms.

## 6. Dates and submission
Questions are due by email by noon on Thursday, March 4, 2027. Proposals must be uploaded as a single PDF file to the City's bid portal by 4:00 p.m. on Thursday, March 11, 2027. The portal does not accept late files. Paper proposals will not be accepted. There is no pre-proposal meeting. Site visits may be arranged by appointment.

No bid bond or performance bond is required for this contract.
