## Notice
Bids due: Thursday, March 25, 2027, 3:00 p.m. Pre-bid conference: Tuesday, March 9, 2027, 10:00 a.m. Engineer's estimate: $620,000 to $700,000. NAICS 238220.

## 1. Description of work
Hollis County will receive sealed bids for the replacement of one 200-ton air-cooled chiller on the roof of the Hollis County Justice Center, 75 Court Street, Hollis, Utah. The work includes removal and disposal of the existing chiller, recovery of refrigerant, structural modifications to the existing roof curb, a new chiller, chilled water piping connections, electrical connections, controls integration with the building automation system, and start-up.

The Justice Center includes the county jail and courtrooms and stays in operation at all times. Cooling may be interrupted for no more than 48 hours in total.

## 2. Pre-bid conference
A pre-bid conference and roof visit will be held on Tuesday, March 9, 2027 at 10:00 a.m. Meet in the lobby of the Justice Center. Bidders are encouraged to attend. Those attending must bring government-issued photo identification to enter the secure area.

## 3. Questions
Questions must be submitted in writing by Friday, March 12, 2027.

## 4. Submission of bids
Sealed bids must be delivered to the Hollis County Clerk, 75 Court Street, Room 104, Hollis, no later than 2:00 p.m. on Thursday, March 25, 2027. Each bid must include:

- the Bid Proposal Form;
- bid security of 5 percent of the total bid, as a bid bond or certified check;
- the Statement of Bidder's Qualifications, showing at least two chiller replacements of 150 tons or larger completed in the last five years;
- proof of a Utah S350 HVAC Contractor license;
- a signed acknowledgment of the Jail Security Rules in Appendix C.

## 5. Award
The contract will be awarded to the lowest responsive and responsible bidder. The county is open to all bidders and applies no preference or set-aside. The successful bidder must furnish performance and payment bonds of 100 percent of the contract amount and proof of insurance within 14 days of award.

## 6. Time for completion
The new chiller must be operating by May 28, 2027.
