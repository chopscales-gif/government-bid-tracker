title: Mechanical Systems Upgrade, Tolliver Mesa High School
agency: Tolliver Mesa County School District
number: RFP 27-002
type: Request for Proposals
issued: February 24, 2027

## 1. Summary
Tolliver Mesa County School District requests proposals from mechanical contractors for the replacement of the heating and cooling systems in the 1978 classroom building at Tolliver Mesa High School, 600 East Canyon Road, Tolliver Mesa, Utah. The project replaces 38 unit ventilators, the heating water boilers and pumps, and adds a building automation system.

The construction budget is $900,000 to $1,100,000. NAICS 238220. The District welcomes proposals from all qualified contractors.

## 2. Selection
This is a best-value selection, not a low-bid selection. Proposals will be scored on price (40 percent), the proposed schedule and plan for working in an occupied school (25 percent), experience of the project manager and superintendent (20 percent), and past performance on school projects (15 percent).

## 3. Mandatory site visit
A mandatory pre-proposal meeting and site visit will be held at the high school on Wednesday, March 17, 2027 at 1:00 p.m. Proposals will be accepted only from firms that attend.

## 4. Proposal requirements
Submit one original and four copies, plus one electronic copy on a USB drive. The proposal must include a management plan, a schedule, resumes for the project manager and superintendent, a list of school projects completed since 2020, a statement of bonding capacity from the firm's surety, and the Cost Proposal Form in a separate sealed envelope.

A proposal bond of 5 percent of the proposed price is required. The firm must hold a Utah S350 HVAC Contractor license.

## 5. Dates
Questions due: Monday, March 22, 2027. Proposals due: Friday, April 2, 2027, 2:00 p.m., District Office, 45 North Main Street, Tolliver Mesa.

## 6. Housing
Tolliver Mesa is a remote community. Contractors are responsible for lodging for their crews. The District has no housing to offer.
