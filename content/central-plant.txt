## 1. Project
Great Basin Technical College invites bids from mechanical contractors, acting as prime contractor, for the replacement of the campus central plant at 1500 College Way, Tanner Flat, Utah. The project includes two 400-ton water-cooled chillers, cooling towers, three hot water boilers, pumps, a new plant control system, electrical service upgrades, and temporary heating and cooling for the campus while the work is done.

The estimated construction cost is $4,200,000 to $4,800,000. NAICS 238220. The solicitation is open to all bidders.

## 2. Prequalification
Only bidders prequalified by the College may bid. A prequalification statement, including a letter from the bidder's surety confirming that the bidder can be bonded for a single project of at least $5,000,000, must be submitted by Friday, March 12, 2027.

## 3. Schedule
Mandatory pre-bid meeting: Tuesday, March 16, 2027, 10:00 a.m., Facilities Building. Questions due: Wednesday, March 24, 2027. Bids due: Thursday, April 8, 2027, 2:00 p.m., Purchasing Office.

## 4. Bids
Bids must include the Bid Form, a 5 percent bid bond, a list of proposed subcontractors for electrical and controls work, and the project manager's resume. The bidder must hold a Utah S350 HVAC Contractor license.

## 5. Bonds
Performance and payment bonds for 100 percent of the contract price are required.

Purchasing Office, purchasing@greatbasintech.example.
