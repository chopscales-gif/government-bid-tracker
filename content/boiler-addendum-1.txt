title: Addendum No. 1 to IFB 27-014, Boiler Replacement, Larkmoor Elementary School
agency: Cedar Bench School District
number: IFB 27-014, Addendum No. 1
type: Addendum
issued: March 1, 2027

## 1. Purpose
This addendum changes the Invitation for Bids issued on February 16, 2027. It forms part of the bid documents. Bidders must acknowledge it on the Bid Form. A bid that does not acknowledge this addendum may be rejected.

## 2. Change to the bid due date
The Board of Education has moved its March meeting forward by one week. To allow the award to be considered at that meeting, the date in Section 2 for receipt of sealed bids is changed from Thursday, March 18, 2027 to Monday, March 15, 2027. The time, 2:00 p.m., and the place are unchanged.

The deadline for written questions is not changed.

## 3. Bid security now required
The first sentence of Section 5 is deleted and replaced with the following: Each bid must be accompanied by bid security in the amount of 5 percent of the bid price, in the form of a bid bond from a surety licensed in Utah or a cashier's check payable to Cedar Bench School District. A bid submitted without bid security will be rejected.

## 4. Clarification
In response to a question from a bidder: boilers from a manufacturer other than the one named in the specifications will be considered if a written request for approval of an equal, with product data, is received by the Purchasing Office by 5:00 p.m. on Monday, March 8, 2027.

All other terms of the Invitation for Bids remain the same.
