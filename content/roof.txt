title: Roof Replacement, Public Works Building
agency: Hollis County
number: IFB HC-2027-12
type: Invitation for Bids
issued: February 25, 2027

## 1. Description of work
Hollis County will receive sealed bids to remove the existing built-up roof on the Public Works Building, 310 Gravel Pit Road, Hollis, Utah, and install a new 60-mil single-ply membrane roof system with tapered insulation, flashings and a 20-year manufacturer's warranty. The roof area is about 22,000 square feet. The work includes disconnecting and resetting four rooftop exhaust fans.

Engineer's estimate: $330,000. NAICS 238160, Roofing Contractors. No set-aside applies.

## 2. Bidder requirements
Bidders must hold a Utah S280 Roofing Contractor license and must be approved by the membrane manufacturer to install the specified system.

## 3. Dates
Pre-bid meeting: Thursday, March 11, 2027, 10:00 a.m., at the building. Attendance is optional. Bids due: Wednesday, March 24, 2027, 2:00 p.m., Hollis County Clerk, 75 Court Street, Room 104, Hollis.

## 4. Bid contents
The Bid Proposal Form, bid security of 5 percent, the manufacturer's letter of approval, and proof of license.

Wendell Achterberg, County Facilities Director, facilities@holliscounty.example.
