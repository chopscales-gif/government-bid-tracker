title: Water Heater Replacement, Quarry Bend Recreation Center
agency: Town of Quarry Bend
number: RFQ 27-03
type: Request for Quotations
issued: February 26, 2027

## 1. Request
The Town of Quarry Bend requests written quotations to replace two 100-gallon gas commercial water heaters and the recirculation pump serving the locker rooms and kitchen at the Quarry Bend Recreation Center, 55 Granary Road, Quarry Bend, Utah. The work must be done while the pool is closed for its annual maintenance, April 12 to April 23, 2027.

The Town expects the cost to be about $48,000.

## 2. Small business limitation
Under the Town's Small Purchase Policy, purchases under $75,000 are limited to small businesses. To be considered, a firm must certify on the Quotation Form that it is a small business, meaning that it has fewer than 100 employees.

## 3. Requirements
The firm must hold a Utah S210 General Plumbing Contractor license or a Utah S350 HVAC Contractor license. NAICS code: 238220.

Quotations must be submitted on the Town's Quotation Form and must include the make and model of the proposed water heaters and the length of the manufacturer's warranty.

No bonds are required. The selected firm must provide proof of liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage before starting work.

## 4. Due date
Quotations are due by 5:00 p.m. on Tuesday, March 16, 2027, by email to the Town Clerk. The Town will award to the lowest quotation that meets these requirements.

To see the mechanical room before quoting, call the Recreation Center manager at (801) 555-0119.
