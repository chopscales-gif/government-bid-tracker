title: Furnace Replacement, Fire Station 2 (Urgent)
agency: City of Marlow Springs
number: Q-27-019
type: Request for Quotations
issued: February 26, 2027

## 1. What is needed
The heat exchanger in the furnace serving the living quarters at Fire Station 2, 918 Willow Street, Marlow Springs, has cracked and the furnace has been shut off. The station is being heated by temporary electric heaters. The City needs a quotation to remove the existing 120,000 BTU gas furnace and install a new furnace of the same capacity, with a new flue and thermostat.

Because of the urgency, the City is using its expedited small purchase procedure. The City expects the cost to be under $30,000.

## 2. Deadline
Quotations must be received by email by 12:00 noon on Friday, March 5, 2027. The City intends to issue a purchase order the same afternoon. Work must be complete within ten days of the purchase order.

## 3. Requirements
The contractor must hold a Utah S350 HVAC Contractor license. The quotation must state a fixed price, the make and model of the furnace, and the date the contractor can start. No bonds are required. A certificate of insurance must be on file with the City before work starts.

Contractors may visit the station any day between 8:00 a.m. and 5:00 p.m. Ask for the duty captain.
