## 1. Pre-bid conference is now mandatory and has moved
Because access to the roof passes through the secure part of the jail, the County will allow only one roof visit. The pre-bid conference described in Section 2 is now mandatory. It is moved from Tuesday, March 9, 2027 at 10:00 a.m. to Monday, March 8, 2027 at 9:00 a.m., in the lobby of the Justice Center. A bid from a firm that did not sign the attendance sheet will not be opened.

Each firm may bring no more than two people. Names must be sent to the Facilities Director by noon on Friday, March 5, 2027 so that security clearance can be arranged.

## 2. Crane and lift plan
Each bid must now include a written crane and lift plan showing where the crane will be set up on Court Street, the weight of the heaviest lift, and the proposed date and hours of the street closure.

## 3. Time of bid opening
The notice at the top of the Invitation for Bids gave the time bids are due as 3:00 p.m. That was an error. Bids are due at 2:00 p.m. on Thursday, March 25, 2027, as stated in Section 4.

## 4. Questions
The deadline for written questions is extended to Tuesday, March 16, 2027.

This addendum must be acknowledged on the Bid Proposal Form.
