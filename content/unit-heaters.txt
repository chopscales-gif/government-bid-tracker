title: Gas Unit Heater Replacement, North Maintenance Garage
agency: Bonneville Valley Transit District
number: IFB BV-27-031
type: Invitation for Bids
issued: February 1, 2027

## 1. Scope
The Bonneville Valley Transit District requests bids to remove fourteen gas-fired unit heaters in the bus bays of the North Maintenance Garage, 2250 Depot Street, Fallon Heights, Utah, and to furnish and install fourteen new separated-combustion unit heaters with thermostats, venting and gas connections.

The estimated cost is $140,000. NAICS code 238220 applies. This solicitation is not set aside.

## 2. Schedule
A site visit will be held Tuesday, February 9, 2027 at 9:00 a.m. Attendance is optional. Questions are due Friday, February 12, 2027. Bids are due Thursday, February 25, 2027 at 2:00 p.m. at the District Procurement Office, 2250 Depot Street, Fallon Heights.

The garage operates 24 hours a day. Work must be done one bay at a time and completed within 60 calendar days of the notice to proceed.

## 3. Requirements
The bidder must hold a Utah S350 HVAC Contractor license. Each bid must include the Bid Form, a bid bond for 5 percent of the bid price, the Non-Collusion Affidavit, and manufacturer's data sheets for the proposed heaters.

The successful bidder must provide performance and payment bonds for the full contract price.
